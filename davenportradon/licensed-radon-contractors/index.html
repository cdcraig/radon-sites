Why isn't every company that advertises here on the list?
Because this list is the state credential roster, not an advertising directory. Some companies advertising radon work locally are not currently credentialed for mitigation in this state — verify the certification number before hiring anyone.
Do you charge companies to be listed?
No. The roster above is public state data reproduced with certification numbers intact. The featured recommendation at the top is how this site is supported.
What should I ask on the first call?
Suction points, pipe route, fan model, retest included, written quote. Those five answers let you compare bids directly and tell you quickly who diagnoses before pricing.
